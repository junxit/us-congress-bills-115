# H.R. 6529

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds by the Department of Health and Human Services to award a grant to study cigar flavors.

## § 1. Limitation on the use of funds

No Federal funds may be used by the Department of Health and Human Services to award a grant to study cigar flavors.
