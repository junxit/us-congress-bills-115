# H.R. 3957

> To repeal title VIII of the Higher Education Opportunity Act and title VIII of the Higher Education Act of 1965.

## § 1. Repeals of additional education programs

- **(a)** *Higher Education Opportunity Act* Title VIII of the Higher Education Opportunity Act (Public Law 110–315) is repealed.
- **(b)** *Higher Education Act of 1965* Title VIII of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1161a et seq.) is repealed.
