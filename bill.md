# H.R. 7265

> To amend title 51, United States Code, to extend the authority of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration to enter into leases of non-excess property of the Administration.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “NASA Enhanced Use Leasing Extension Act of 2018”.

## § 2. Extension of authority to enter into leases of non-excess property of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration

Section 20145(g) of title 51, United States Code, is amended by striking “December 31, 2018” and inserting “December 31, 2019”.
