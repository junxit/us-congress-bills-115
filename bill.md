# H.R. 4776

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize a program of partnerships for State and regional hospital preparedness to improve surge capacity.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Hospital Preparedness Reauthorization Act of 2018”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of partnerships for State and regional hospital preparedness To improve surge capacity

Section 319C–2(j)(1) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 247d–3b(j)(1)) is amended by striking “$374,700,000 for each of fiscal years 2014 through 2018” and inserting “$515,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2019 through 2023”.
