# H.R. 5899

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize school-based health centers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “School-Based Health Centers Reauthorization Act of 2018”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of school-based health centers

Section 399Z–1(l) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 280h–5(l)) is amended by striking “2010 through 2014” and inserting “2018 through 2023”.
