# H.R. 5743

> To prohibit the National Science Foundation from conducting certain studies on relationships between Members of Congress with respect to the sharing of guidance and informative documents.

## § 1. Prohibition on use of funds for certain studies by the National Science Foundation

No Federal funds may be used by the National Science Foundation to conduct a study on—

- **(1)** how Members of Congress set priorities within the constraints of the contemporary legislature;
- **(2)** the relationships between Members of Congress with respect to the sharing of guidance and informative documents; or
- **(3)** the impact of such guidance and informative documents, including dear colleague letters, sent between Members of Congress.
