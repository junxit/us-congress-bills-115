# H.R. 2103

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to include the Frye Scholarship in the Yellow Ribbon G.I. Education Enhancement Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Julian Woods Yellow Ribbon Program Expansion Act”.

## § 2. Inclusion of Frye Scholarship recipients in Yellow Ribbon G.I. Education Enhancement Program

Section 3317(a) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “paragraphs (1) and (2) of section 3311(b)” and inserting “paragraphs (1), (2), and (9) of section 3311(b) of this title”.
