# H.R. 3228

> To require the President to provide frequent press briefings covering the official business of the President to the White House press corps.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Free Press Act of 2017”.

## § 2. White House press corps

The President shall provide for video recorded press briefings covering the official business of the President to the White House press corps not less than 2 times per week.
