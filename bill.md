# H. RES. 975

> Expressing the sense of the House of Representatives that Americans have a right to fair representation and that America’s democratic institutions are in urgent need of repair to provide greater responsiveness and accountability to the people through critical reforms that empower the American voter, strengthen our Nation’s ethics laws, and fix our broken campaign finance system.

## § 1. Short title

This resolution may be cited as the “By the People Resolution”.

## § 2. Purpose

It is the purpose of this resolution to express the sense of the House of Representatives that Congress should advance a comprehensive set of political reforms to restore trust in, and integrity to, our institutions of democracy. Such reforms will—

- **(1)** empower the American voter;
- **(2)** strengthen our Nation’s ethics laws; and
- **(3)** fix our broken campaign finance system.
