# H.R. 127

> To amend title 49, United States Code, with respect to urbanized area formula grants, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Transportation for Heroes Act of 2017”.

## § 2. Urbanized area formula grants

Section 5307(c)(1)(D) of title 49, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in clause (ii) by striking “and” at the end;
- **(2)** in clause (iii) by inserting “and” after the semicolon at the end; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(iv)** veteran (as such term is defined in section 101 of title 38);
