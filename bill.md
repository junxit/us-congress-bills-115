# H.R. 1389

> To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to provide States with flexibility to provide care coordination under Medicaid for the most vulnerable through managed care.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Increasing State Flexibility in Care Coordination for the Most Vulnerable Act of 2017”.

## § 2. State flexibility to provide care coordination under Medicaid for the most vulnerable through managed care

Section 1932(a)(2) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396u–2(a)(2)) is amended by striking subparagraphs (A) and (B).
