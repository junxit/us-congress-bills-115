# H.R. 7243

> To amend Public Law 115–217 to change the address of the postal facility designated by such Public Law in honor of Sergeant First Class Alwyn Crendall Cashe, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Amendment to change address of postal facility designated by Public Law 115–217

In section 1(a) of Public Law 115–217, strike “567 East Franklin Street” and insert “83 Geneva Drive”.
