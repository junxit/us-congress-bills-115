# H.R. 6862

> To reauthorize the Congressional Award Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Congressional Award Program Reauthorization Act of 2018”.

## § 2. Reauthorization

Section 108 of the Congressional Award Act (2 U.S.C. 808) is amended by striking “October 1, 2018” and inserting “October 1, 2023”.

## § 3. Effective Date

This Act shall take effect as of October 1, 2018.
