# H.R. 6253

> To prohibit the Department of Health and Human Services from using any Federal funds to conduct or support a video contest on the Internet or by means of other media.

## § 1. Prohibition against HHS funding video contests

The Department of Health and Human Services shall not use any Federal funds to conduct or support a video contest on the Internet or by means of other media, including any such contest on YouTube.
