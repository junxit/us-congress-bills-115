# S. 3593

> To amend the Family and Medical Leave Act of 1993, to repeal certain limits on leave for a husband and wife employed by the same employer.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fair Access for Individuals to Receive Leave Act” or the “FAIR Leave Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of certain time limitations on leave for spouses

Section 102(f) of the Family and Medical Leave Act of 1993 (29 U.S.C. 2612(f)) is repealed.
