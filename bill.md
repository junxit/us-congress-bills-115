# H.R. 4105

> To amend title XX of the Social Security Act to extend the health professions workforce demonstration project.

## § 1. Extension of the health professions workforce demonstration project

Section 2008(c)(1) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1397g(c)(1)) is amended by striking “2017” and inserting “2022”.
