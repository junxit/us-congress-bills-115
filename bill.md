# S. 3554

> To extend the effective date for the sunset for collateral requirements for Small Business Administration disaster loans.

## § 1. Extension of sunset for collateral requirements for SBA disaster loans

Section 2102(b) of the RISE After Disaster Act of 2015 (15 U.S.C. 636 note) is amended, in the matter preceding paragraph (1), by striking “3 years” and inserting “4 years”.
