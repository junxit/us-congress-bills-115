# H.R. 4630

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to ensure military airports are eligible for certain grant funds.

## § 1. Former military airports

Section 47118(a) of title 49, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1)(C) by striking “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (2) by striking the period at the end and inserting “; or”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(3)** the airport is—
  >   - **(A)** a former military installation; and
  >   - **(B)** a primary airport.
