# S. 2238

> To amend the Ohio & Erie Canal National Heritage Canalway Act of 1996 to repeal the funding limitation.

## § 1. Ohio & Erie National Heritage Canalway

Section 810(a) of the Ohio & Erie National Heritage Canalway Act of 1996 (Public Law 104–333; 110 Stat. 4275; 122 Stat. 826) is amended by striking the second sentence.
