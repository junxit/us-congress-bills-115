# H.R. 3493

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to authorize grants for local care corps programs through which qualified volunteers provide care, companionship, and other services to seniors and individuals in need, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Care Corps Demonstration Act of 2017”.

## § 2. Grants for local care corps programs

Part D of title III of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254b et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating the second subpart XI (relating to a community-based collaborative care network program) as subpart XII;
