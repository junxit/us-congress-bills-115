# H.R. 7162

> For the relief of David Beasley.

## § 1. Presumption of service connection for ankylosing spondylitis with regards to David Beasley

With regards to David Beasley, the Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall treat ankylosing spondylitis as a disease specified in section 1116(a)(2) of title 38, United States Code.
