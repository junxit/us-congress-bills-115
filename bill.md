# H.R. 6158

> To amend the Federal Deposit Insurance Act to exclude affiliates and subsidiaries of insured depository institutions in the definition of deposit broker, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Brokered Deposit Affiliate-Subsidiary Modernization Act of 2018”.

## § 2. Exclusion of affiliates and subsidiaries of insured depository institutions in the definition of deposit broker

Section 29(g) of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (12 U.S.C. 1831f(g)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2)—
  - **(A)** in subparagraph (A), by inserting “or any affiliate or subsidiary of such insured depository institution” after “insured depository institution”; and
  - **(B)** in subparagraph (B), by inserting “or any affiliate or subsidiary of such insured depository institution” after “insured depository institution”; and
- **(2)** by striking paragraph (4) and inserting the following:
  > - **(4)** *Employee* For purposes of this subsection, the term employee—
  >   - **(A)** means an individual who receives compensation in any form from an insured depository institution or an affiliate or subsidiary of such insured depository institution; and
  >   - **(B)** includes a registered representative of a broker or dealer that is an affiliate or subsidiary of an insured depository institution.
