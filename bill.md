# S. 2053

> To amend the Agricultural Act of 2014 to increase the maximum amount of assistance authorized under supplemental agricultural disaster assistance programs.

## § 1. Payment limitation for supplemental agricultural disaster assistance

Section 1501(f)(2) of the Agricultural Act of 2014 (7 U.S.C. 9081(f)(2)) is amended by striking “$125,000” and inserting “$250,000”.
