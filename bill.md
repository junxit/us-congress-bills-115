# H.R. 6906

> To designate the portion of Interstate Route 57 that is located in Illinois as the “Barack Obama Highway”, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Designation

The portion of Interstate Route 57 that is located in Illinois shall be known and designated as the “Barack Obama Highway”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the portion of Interstate Route 57 referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Barack Obama Highway”.
