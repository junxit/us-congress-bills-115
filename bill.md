# S. 2156

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to provide for fairness in hospital payments under the Medicare program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Hospital Payment Fairness Act of 2017”.

## § 2. Sunset of Medicare hospital payment provision

Section 3141 of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (42 U.S.C. 1395ww note) is amended by inserting “and before April 1, 2018,” after “October 1, 2010,”.
