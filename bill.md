# H.R. 2617

> To provide first-time, low-level, nonviolent simple possession offenders under age 25 an opportunity to expunge that conviction after successful completion of court-imposed probation.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Renew Act of 2017”.

## § 2. Lowering the age for expungement of certain convictions for simple possession of controlled substances by nonviolent young offenders

Section 3607(c) of title 18, United States Code, is amended by striking “less than twenty-one” and inserting “less than twenty-five”.
