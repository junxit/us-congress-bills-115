# S. 2758

> To amend title 36, United States Code, to provide for the display of the National League of Families POW/MIA flag at the World War I Memorials.

## § 1. Display of National League of Families POW/MIA flag at the World War I Memorials

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (d)(3) of section 902 of title 36, United States Code, is amended by striking “The World War II Memorial,” and inserting “The World War I Memorials, the World War II Memorial,”.
- **(b)** *Days for display* Subsection (c)(2)(A) of such section is amended by inserting “the World War I Memorials,” before “the World War II Memorial,”.
