# H.R. 5250

> To reauthorize the Rural Economic Area Partnership Zones Program through fiscal year 2023.

## § 1. Reauthorization of the Rural Economic Area Partnership Zones Program

Section 310B(j) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1932(j)) is amended by striking “2018” and inserting “2023”.
