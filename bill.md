# H.R. 3921

> To extend funding for the Children’s Health Insurance Program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Helping Ensure Access for Little Ones, Toddlers, and Hopeful Youth by Keeping Insurance Delivery Stable Act of 2017” or the “HEALTHY KIDS Act”.
