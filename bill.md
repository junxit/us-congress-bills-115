# H.R. 5489

> To institute reforms to the program of block grants to States for temporary assistance for needy families and the supplemental nutrition assistance program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Hope Opportunity and Prosperity for Everyone Act” or the “Hope Act”.
