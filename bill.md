# H.R. 5872

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to increase the limitation on the deduction for State and local taxes.

## § 1. Increase in limitation on deduction for State and local taxes

- **(a)** *In general* Section 164(b)(6)(B) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “$10,000 ($5,000” and inserting “$20,000 ($10,000”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall take effect as if included in the amendments made by section 11042 of Public Law 115–97.
