# H.R. 7200

> To extend indemnity for wildfires and hurricanes, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Wildfires and Hurricanes Indemnity Program Extension Act of 2018”.

## § 2. Extension

Title I of subdivision 1 of division B of the Bipartisan Budget Act of 2018 (Public Law 115–123) is amended under the heading “Department of Agriculture—Agricultural Programs—Processing, Research and Marketing—Office of the Secretary”—

- **(1)** by striking “December 31, 2019” and inserting “December 31, 2020”;
- **(2)** by inserting “Florence, Michael,” after “Maria,”; and
- **(3)** by inserting “or 2018” after “occurring in calendar year 2017”.
