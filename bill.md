# H.R. 293

> To extend the authorization of appropriations to the Department of Veterans Affairs for purposes of awarding grants to veterans service organizations for the transportation of highly rural veterans.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Highly Rural Veteran Transportation Program Extension Act”.

## § 2. Extension of authority to make grants to Veterans Service Organizations for transportation of highly rural veterans

Section 307(d) of the Caregivers and Veterans Omnibus Health Services Act of 2010 (Public Law 111–163; 124 Stat. 1154; 38 U.S.C. 1710 note), as most recently amended by section 106 of the Department of Veterans Affairs Expiring Authorities Act of 2016 (Public Law 114–228; 130 Stat. 937), is amended by striking “2017” and inserting “2018”.
