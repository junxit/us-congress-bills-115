# S. 3415

> To extend, for a period of two years, the authority for operation of the Department of Veterans Affairs regional office in Manila, the Republic of the Philippines.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Keeping Our Commitment to Overseas Veterans Act of 2018”.

## § 2. Extension of authority for operation of the Department of Veterans Affairs regional office in Manila, the Republic of the Philippines

Section 315(b) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “September 30, 2018” and inserting “September 30, 2020”.
