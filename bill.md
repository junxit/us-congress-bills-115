# H.R. 5325

> To amend title 40, United States Code, to reauthorize regional economic and infrastructure development programs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Southwest Border Regional Commission Reauthorization Act of 2018”.

## § 2. Authorization of appropriations

Section 15751(a) of title 40, United States Code, is amended by striking “2018” and inserting “2023”.
