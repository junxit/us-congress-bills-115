# H.R. 6338

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds by the National Endowment for the Arts to award a grant for South Dakota State University’s “Historic Hobo Day”.

## § 1. Limitation on the use of funds

No Federal funds may be used by the National Endowment for the Arts to award a grant for South Dakota State University’s “Historic Hobo Day”.
