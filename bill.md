# H.R. 3369

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 225 North Main Street in Spring Lake, North Carolina, as the “Howard B. Pate, Jr. Post Office”.

## § 1. Howard B. Pate, Jr. Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 225 North Main Street in Spring Lake, North Carolina, shall be known and designated as the “Howard B. Pate, Jr. Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Howard B. Pate, Jr. Post Office”.
