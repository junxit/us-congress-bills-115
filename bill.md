# H.R. 5827

> To extend the authorization for the National Dam Safety Program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Dam Safety Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Extension of authorization of dam safety programs

Section 14 of the National Dam Safety Program Act (33 U.S.C. 467j) is amended by striking “2015 through 2019” each place it appears and inserting “2015 through 2024”.
