# H.R. 5151

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize a grant program improving treatment for pregnant and postpartum women.

## § 1. Improving treatment for pregnant and postpartum women

Section 508(s) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 290bb–1(s)) is amended in the first sentence by inserting before the period the following: “, and $100,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2019 through 2023”.
