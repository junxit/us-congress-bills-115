# H.R. 7074

> To amend the Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976 to reauthorize grazing advisory boards.

## § 1. Grazing advisory boards reauthorized

- **(a)** *Short title* This section may be cited as the “Grazing Advisory Board Reauthorization Act”.
- **(b)** *Reauthorization of grazing advisory boards* Section 403(f) of the Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976 (43 U.S.C. 1753(f)) is amended by striking “December 31, 1985” and inserting “on the date that is 10 years after the date of the enactment of the Grazing Advisory Board Reauthorization Act”.
