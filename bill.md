# H. RES. 1047

> Expressing the profound sorrow of the House of Representatives on the death of the Honorable John Sidney McCain, III, a Senator from the State of Arizona.

That the House has heard with profound sorrow of the death of the Honorable John Sidney McCain, III, a Senator from the State of Arizona.

That the Clerk communicate these resolutions to the Senate and transmit a copy thereof to the family of the deceased.

That when the House adjourns today, it adjourn as a further mark of respect to the memory of the deceased Senator.
