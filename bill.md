# H.R. 6842

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide an automatic 60-day delay for certain internal revenue requirements in the case of taxpayers in an area with respect to which a federally declared disaster is requested.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Disaster Certainty Act”.

## § 2. Automatic 60-day delay for taxpayers in area with respect to which federally declared disaster is requested

- **(a)** *In general* Section 7508A(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end the following:
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply with respect to disasters declared in taxable years beginning after December 31, 2017.
