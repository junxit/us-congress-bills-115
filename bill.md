# H.R. 3667

> To amend the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act to provide for a 5-year extension of the appropriate technology transfer for rural areas program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Veteran and Beginning Farmers Assistance Act”.

## § 2. Extension of appropriate technology transfer for rural areas program

Section 310B(i)(4) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1932(i)(4)) is amended by striking “2018” and inserting “2023”.
