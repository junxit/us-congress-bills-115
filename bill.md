# H.R. 1754

> To amend the Federal Trade Commission Act to clarify the scope of the exception for common carriers.

## § 1. Clarification of scope of common carrier exception

The Federal Trade Commission Act (15 U.S.C. 41 et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 5(a)(2), by inserting “insofar as they are” after “common carriers”; and
- **(2)** in section 6—
  - **(A)** by inserting “insofar as they are” after “common carriers” each place it appears; and
  - **(B)** by inserting “insofar as it is” after “common carrier” each place it appears.
