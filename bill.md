# H.R. 1336

> To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to provide States with flexibility in providing choice of coverage through managed care under Medicaid.

## § 1. State flexibility to provide choice of coverage in managed care

Paragraph (3) of section 1932(a) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396u–2(a)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(3)** *Choice of coverage* A State may, at its option, permit an individual to choose a managed care entity from two or more such entities that meet the applicable requirements of this section, and of section 1903(m) or section 1905(t).
