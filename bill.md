# H.R. 5234

> To amend the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 to expand opportunities for algae-based research, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Expanding opportunities for algae-based research

Section 9011(a)(6)(C) of the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 (7 U.S.C. 8111(a)(6)(C)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking clause (iv); and
- **(2)** by redesignating clauses (v) through (vii) as clauses (iv) through (vi), respectively.
