# H.R. 5895

> Making appropriations for energy and water development and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2019, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Energy and Water, Legislative Branch, and Military Construction and Veterans Affairs Appropriations Act, 2019”.
