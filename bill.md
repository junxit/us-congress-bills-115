# H.R. 4970

> To require the creation and maintenance of a MadeInAmerica.gov website.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “MadeInAmerica.gov Act”.

## § 2. Made in America website

- **(a)** *Website creation* Not later than 2 years after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Commerce shall create and maintain a “MadeInAmerica.gov” website where consumers can find information about products that are made in America.
- **(b)** *Regulations* The Secretary shall issue such regulations as are necessary to implement this Act.
