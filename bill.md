# H. RES. 800

> Amending the Rules of the House of Representatives to require the House to replace the Speaker on the first day of the second session of a Congress if the most recent fiscal year ended with a significant Federal budget deficit, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Replacement of Speaker at beginning of second session in case of significant Federal budget deficit

- **(a)** *Replacement* Rule I of the Rules of the House of Representatives is amended by adding at the end the following new clause:
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to the One Hundred Sixteenth Congress and each succeeding Congress.
