# S. 2991

> To amend the Rural Electrification Act of 1936 to provide that cybersecurity and grid security improvements are eligible for electric loans and loan guarantees under that Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Promoting Cybersecurity for Rural Electric Utilities Act”.

## § 2. Cybersecurity and grid security improvements

Title III of the Rural Electrification Act of 1936 (7 U.S.C. 931 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> ## § 319. Cybersecurity and grid security improvements
>
> The Secretary may make or guarantee loans under this title and title I for cybersecurity and grid security improvements.
