# H.R. 4741

> To require the Postmaster General to designate a single, unique ZIP code for particular communities, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Designation of zip codes

Not later than 60 days after the date of enactment of this section, the Postmaster General of the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP code for, as nearly as practicable, the communities of Flanders, Northampton, and Riverside in the Town of Southampton, New York.
