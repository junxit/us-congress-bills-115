# H.R. 4541

> To extend funding for certain public health programs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Continuing Community Health and Medical Professional Programs to Improve Our Nation and Keep Insurance Delivery Stable Act of 2017” or the “CHAMPION KIDS Act of 2017”.
