# H. RES. 569

> Providing for the concurrence by the House in the Senate amendment to H.R. 2266, with an amendment.

That upon the adoption of this resolution the House shall be considered to have taken from the Speaker's table the bill, H.R. 2266, with the Senate amendment thereto, and to have concurred in the Senate amendment with the following amendment:

In lieu of the matter proposed to be inserted by the Senate amendment, insert the following:

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Additional Supplemental Appropriations for Disaster Relief Requirements Act, 2017”.
