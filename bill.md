# H.R. 6893

> To amend the Overtime Pay for Protective Services Act of 2016 to extend the Secret Service overtime pay exception through 2019, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Secret Service Overtime Pay Extension Act”.

## § 2. Extension of overtime pay exception through 2019 for protective services

Section 2 of the Overtime Pay for Protective Services Act of 2016 (5 U.S.C. 5547 note), as amended by Public Law 115–160, is further amended—

- **(1)** in the section heading, by striking “2018” and inserting “2019”;
- **(2)** in subsection (a), by striking “or 2018” and inserting “2018, or 2019”; and
- **(3)** in subsection (b)(1), by striking “or 2018” and inserting “2018, or 2019”.
