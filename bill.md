# H.R. 3723

> To extend the National Flood Insurance Program.

## § 1. Extension of national flood insurance program

- **(a)** *Financing* Section 1309(a) of the National Flood Insurance Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 4016(a)) is amended by striking “September 30, 2017” and inserting “September 30, 2019”.
- **(b)** *Program expiration* Section 1319 of the National Flood Insurance Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 4026) is amended by striking “September 30, 2017” and inserting “September 30, 2019”.
