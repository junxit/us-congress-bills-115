# H.R. 1105

> To repeal the rule entitled “Clean Water Rule: Definition of “Waters of the United States””.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop WOTUS Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of rule relating to waters of the United States

The final rule entitled “Clean Water Rule: Definition of “Waters of the United States””, published on June 29, 2015 (80 Fed. Reg. 37054), shall have no force or effect.
