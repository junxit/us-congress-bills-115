# S. 2952

> To amend the Congressional Accountability Act of 1995 to establish protections against congressional sexual harassment and discrimination, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; references in Act; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Congressional Accountability Act of 1995 Reform Act”.
- **(b)** *References in Act* Except as otherwise expressly provided in this Act, wherever an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to or repeal of a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to that section or other provision of the Congressional Accountability Act of 1995 (2 U.S.C. 1301 et seq.).
