# S. 77

> To amend the Agricultural Act of 2014 to repeal the forfeiture rule for peanuts under the nonrecourse marketing assistance loan program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Paying U.S. in Peanuts Act ”.

## § 2. Repeal of special rule for peanuts

Section 1201 of the Agricultural Act of 2014 (7 U.S.C. 9031) is amended by striking subsection (e).
