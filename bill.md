# H.R. 3924

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to extend funding for the special diabetes program for type I diabetes.

## § 1. Extension for special diabetes program for type I diabetes

Section 330B(b)(2)(C) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254c–2(b)(2)(C)) is amended by striking “2017” and inserting “2019”.
