# H. RES. 441

> Amending the Rules of the House of Representatives to prohibit the consideration of any bill or joint resolution until a cost estimate prepared by the Congressional Budget Office has been available to the public, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This resolution may be cited as the “Require a Score Before the Floor Resolution”.
