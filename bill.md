# H.R. 6057

> To amend the Controlled Substances Act to provide for enhanced penalties for trafficking in fentanyl, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Enhanced penalties for trafficking in fentanyl

Section 401(b)(1)(B) of the Controlled Substances Act (21 U.S.C. 841(b)(1)(B)) is amended—

- **(1)** in clause (vi), by striking “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in clause (vii), by striking “or” at the end;
- **(3)** in clause (viii), by adding “or” at the end; and
- **(4)** by inserting after clause (viii) the following:
  > - **(ix)** 2 grams or more of a mixture or substance containing a detectable amount of fentanyl, its salts, optical and geometric isomers, and salts of isomers;
