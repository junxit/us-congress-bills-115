# H.J. RES. 122

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to “Payday, Vehicle Title, and Certain High-Cost Installment Loans”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to “Payday, Vehicle Title, and Certain High-Cost Installment Loans” (published at 82 Fed. Reg. 54472 (November 17, 2017)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
