# H.R. 3373

> To amend the District of Columbia Stadium Act of 1957 to extend for an additional 50 years the lease under which the government of the District of Columbia uses the ground under and the parking facilities associated with Robert F. Kennedy Memorial Stadium.

## § 1. Extension of lease for use of ground and parking facilities of Robert F. Kennedy Stadium by government of the District of Columbia

Section 7(b) of the District of Columbia Stadium Act of 1957 (sec. 3–326(b), D.C. Official Code) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2), by striking “The lease” and inserting “Except as provided in paragraph (3), the lease”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
  > - **(3)** The Secretary of the Interior shall extend the lease authorized by paragraph (1)(B) so that the lease shall be in effect for a total period of 100 years.
