# H.R. 3293

> To amend section 2533a of title 10, United States Code, to add stainless steel flatware to the list of covered items in such section.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Support Procurement Of Our Nation’s Stainless Steel Act of 2017” or the “SPOONSS Act”.

## § 2. Addition of domestically produced stainless steel flatware to the Berry Amendment

- **(a)** *In general* Section 2533a(b) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
  > - **(3)** Stainless steel flatware.

- **(b)** *Effective date* Section 2533a(b)(3) of title 10, United States Code, as added by subsection (a), shall apply with respect to contracts entered into after the date occurring 1 year after the date of the enactment of this Act.
