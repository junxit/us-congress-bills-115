# H.R. 3700

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend qualified zone academy bonds for 4 years and to reduce the private business contribution requirement with respect to such bonds.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Investing in 21st Century Schools Act”.

## § 2. Extension and modification of qualified zone academy bonds

- **(a)** *Extension* Section 54E(c)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “and 2016” and inserting “2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, and 2020”.
- **(b)** *Reduction of private business contribution requirement* Section 54E(b) of such Code is amended by striking “10 percent” and inserting “5 percent”.
- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to obligations issued after the date of the enactment of this Act.
