# H.R. 3370

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend the Yellow Ribbon G.I. Education Enhancement Program to cover recipients of the Marine Gunnery Sergeant John David Fry scholarship, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fry Scholarship Enhancement Act”.

## § 2. Expansion of Yellow Ribbon G.I. Education Enhancement Program

- **(a)** *In general* Section 3317(a) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “paragraphs (1) and (2) of section 3311(b)” and inserting “paragraphs (1), (2), (8), and (9) of section 3311(b) of this title”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect on August 1, 2018.
