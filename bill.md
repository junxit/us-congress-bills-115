# S. 2573

> To amend the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act to reauthorize the appropriate technology transfer for rural areas program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Veteran and Beginning Farmers Technical Assistance Act”.

## § 2. Appropriate technology transfer for rural areas program

Section 310B(i)(4) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1932(i)(4)) is amended by striking “2018” and inserting “2023”.
