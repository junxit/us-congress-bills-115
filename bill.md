# S. 2817

> To clarify that funding for the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board is not subject to the sequester.

## § 1. Clarification

Section 251A of the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985 (2 U.S.C. 901a) does not apply with respect to the funding of the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board.
