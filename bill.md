# H.R. 3680

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to make permanent the authority of the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to provide financial assistance for supportive services for very low-income veteran families in permanent housing.

## § 1. Making permanent authority to provide financial assistance for supportive services for very low-income veteran families in permanent housing

Paragraph (1) of section 2044(e) is amended by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:

> - **(F)** The amounts that are appropriated to carry out such subsections for fiscal year 2018 and each fiscal year thereafter.
