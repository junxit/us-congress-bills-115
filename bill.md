# S. RES. 712

> Authorizing the use of official office funds to purchase the pocket version of the Constitution of the United States.

## § 1. Pocket version of the Constitution of the United States

Section 2(3) of Senate Resolution 294 (96th Congress), agreed to April 29, 1980, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “and copies” and inserting “copies”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “, and copies of the pocket version of the Constitution of the United States published by the Government Publishing Office” after “Historical Society”.
