# S. 948

> To designate as wilderness certain Federal portions of the red rock canyons of the Colorado Plateau and the Great Basin Deserts in the State of Utah for the benefit of present and future generations of people in the United States.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “America’s Red Rock Wilderness Act of 2017”.

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of the Interior, acting through the Bureau of Land Management.
- **(2)** *State* The term State means the State of Utah.
