# H.R. 6656

> To amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 to adjust the formula allocation under the Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance Grant Program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Start Allocating Funds Efficiently to States Act”.

## § 2. Formula allocation under the Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance Grant Program

Section 505(a)(1) of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10156(a)(1)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “50 percent” and inserting “30 percent”; and
- **(2)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “50 percent” and inserting “70 percent”.
