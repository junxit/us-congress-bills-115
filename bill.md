# H.R. 4446

> To amend the Virgin Islands of the United States Centennial Commission Act to extend the expiration date of the Commission, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Amendment

The Virgin Islands of the United States Centennial Commission Act (Public Law 114–224) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 7(b), by striking “January 31, 2018” and inserting “January 31, 2019”; and
- **(2)** in section 10, by striking “September 30, 2018” and inserting “September 30, 2019”.
