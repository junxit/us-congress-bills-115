# H.R. 6113

> To establish new ZIP codes for certain communities, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Establishing new ZIP codes

Not later than September 30, 2019, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP code for, as nearly as practicable, each of the following communities:

- **(1)** Miami Lakes, Florida.
- **(2)** Storey County, Nevada.
- **(3)** Flanders, Northampton, and Riverside in the Town of Southampton, New York.
- **(4)** Ocoee, Florida.
- **(5)** Oakland, Florida.
- **(6)** Glendale, New York.
- **(7)** Village of Estero, Florida.
