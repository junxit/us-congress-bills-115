# S. 525

> To require the United States Postal Service to designate a single, unique ZIP code for particular communities.

## § 1. ZIP codes

Not later than 270 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP code for, as nearly as practicable, the communities of Flanders, Northampton, and Riverside in the Town of Southampton, New York.
