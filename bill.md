# H.R. 768

> To require the United States Postal Service to designate a single, unique ZIP code for Miami Lakes, Florida.

## § 1. ZIP Code for Miami Lakes, Florida

Not later than September 30, 2017, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP code for, as nearly as practicable, for Miami Lakes, Florida.
