# H.R. 4520

> To provide for the extension of the enforcement instruction on supervision requirements for outpatient therapeutic services in critical access and small rural hospitals through 2017.

## § 1. Extension of enforcement instruction on supervision requirements for outpatient therapeutic services in critical access and small rural hospitals through 2017

Section 1 of Public Law 113–198, as amended by section 1 of Public Law 114–112 and section 16004 of Public Law 114–255, is amended—

- **(1)** in the heading, by striking “2016” and inserting “2017”; and
- **(2)** by striking “ and 2016” and inserting “2016, and 2017”.
