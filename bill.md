# S. RES. 467

> Notifying the House of Representatives of the election of a Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate.

That the House of Representatives be notified of the election of the Honorable Michael C. Stenger as Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate.
