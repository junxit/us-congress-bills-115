# S. 688

> To suspend the importation of beef and poultry from Brazil.

## § 1. Suspension of importation of beef and poultry from Brazil

Beef, beef products, poultry, and poultry products imported from Brazil may not enter the United States during the period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act and ending on the date that is 120 days after such date of enactment.
