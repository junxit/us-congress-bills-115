# S.J. RES. 37

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to “Energy Conservation Program: Test Procedures for Compressors”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to “Energy Conservation Program: Test Procedures for Compressors” (82 Fed. Reg. 1052 (January 4, 2017)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
