# H.R. 3156

> To establish the Water Science Centers within the United States Geological Survey.

## § 1. Water Science Centers Established

There are hereby established, within the United States Geological Survey, local Water Science Centers that shall collect and disseminate reliable, impartial, and timely information about the Nation's water resources.
