# H.R. 5437

> To require the Secretary of the Treasury to establish a program for the issuance of identity protection personal identification numbers.

## § 1. Identity protection personal identification numbers

Not later than 5 years after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of the Treasury (or the Secretary’s delegate) shall establish a program to issue, upon the request of any individual, a number which may be used in connection with such individual’s Social Security number (or other identifying information with respect to such individual) to assist the Secretary in verifying such individual’s identity.
