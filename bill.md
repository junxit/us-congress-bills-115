# H. RES. 1099

> Providing for the concurrence by the House in the Senate amendment to H.R. 6, with an amendment.

That upon the adoption of this resolution the House shall be considered to have taken from the Speaker’s table the bill, H.R. 6, with the Senate amendment thereto, and to have concurred in the Senate amendment with the following amendment:

-  In lieu of the matter proposed to be inserted by the amendment of the Senate to the text of the bill, insert the following:
