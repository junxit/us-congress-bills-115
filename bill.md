# H.R. 3496

> To amend the Museum and Library Services Act to authorize grants to support the use of public libraries for community economic development.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Public Library Innovation Space Act”.

## § 2. Authorization of appropriations

Subsection (a) of section 214 of the Museum and Library Services Act (20 U.S.C. 9123(a)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1), by striking “and” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (2), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(3)** to carry out chapter 5, $10,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2018 through 2022.
