# H.R. 5106

> To amend the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 to require issuers to disclose in an annual report any substantial financial relationship with any manufacturer or dealer of firearms or ammunition.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Investor Choice Against Gun Proliferation Act”.

## § 2. Disclosure of substantial financial relationship with any manufacturer or dealer of firearms or ammunition

Section 13 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 78m) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(s)** *Disclosure of substantial financial relationship with manufacturer or dealer of firearms or ammunition* Each issuer required to file an annual or quarterly report under subsection (a) shall annually disclose in such a report any substantial financial relationship such issuer has with any manufacturer or dealer of firearms or ammunition.
