# S. 2116

> To amend the Federal Deposit Insurance Act to increase the asset threshold with respect to the on-site examination of certain insured depository institutions.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Community Lender Exam Act”.

## § 2. Examination requirements

Section 10(d)(4)(A) of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (12 U.S.C. 1820(d)(4)(A)) is amended by striking “$1,000,000,000” and inserting “$3,000,000,000”.
