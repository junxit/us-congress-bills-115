# S. 1946

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow unpopulated census tracts that are contiguous to low-income communities to be treated as low-income communities under the new markets tax credit.

## § 1. Treatment of certain unpopulated census tracts under new markets tax credit

- **(a)** *In general* Section 45D(e)(4)(A) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “is within” and inserting
    > is—
    > - **(i)** within

  - **(2)** by striking “and” at the end and inserting “or”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following new clause:
    > - **(ii)** a census tract with a population of zero, and

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to investments made after the date of the enactment of this Act.
