# H.R. 4762

> To require the United States Postal Service to designate a single, unique ZIP code for particular communities.

## § 1. Zip codes

Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP code for, as nearly as practicable, each of the following communities:

- **(1)** Ocoee, Florida.
- **(2)** Oakland, Florida.
