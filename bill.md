# H.J. RES. 96

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of the Interior relating to “Eastern Interior Proposed Resource Management Plan and Final Environmental Impact Statement”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of the Interior relating to “Eastern Interior Proposed Resource Management Plan and Final Environmental Impact Statement”, for which notice of availability was published in the Federal Register (81 Fed. Reg. 50009 (July 29, 2016)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
