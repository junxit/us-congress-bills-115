# H.R. 998

> To provide for the establishment of a process for the review of rules and sets of rules, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Searching for and Cutting Regulations that are Unnecessarily Burdensome Act” or as the “SCRUB Act” .
