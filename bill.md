# H.R. 6479

> To amend title XXVII of the Public Health Service Act to include short-term limited duration plans in the definition of individual health insurance coverage.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Junk Health Plans Act of 2018”.

## § 2. Preventing junk plans and continuing protections for consumers with preexisting conditions

- **(a)** *Including short-Term limited duration insurance as individual health insurance coverage* Section 2791(b)(5) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300gg–91(b)(5)) is amended by striking “but does not include short-term limited duration insurance” and inserting “including short-term limited duration insurance”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply with respect to plan years beginning after December 31, 2018.
