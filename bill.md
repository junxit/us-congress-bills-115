# S. 1316

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to provide for a one-year extension of the suicide prevention and resilience program for the National Guard and Reserves.

## § 1. One-year extension of suicide prevention and resilience program for the National Guard and Reserves

Section 10219(g) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking “October 1, 2018” and inserting “October 1, 2019”.
