# H.R. 7280

> To direct the Architect of the Capitol to install a commemorative plaque next to each office previously used by a Member of Congress who became President of the United States, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Commemorative plaque for offices previously used by Members of Congress who became President

- **(a)** *In general* Not later than 90 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Architect of the Capitol shall design, procure, prepare, and install a plaque described in subsection (b) for display immediately next to the front door of each office that—
  - **(1)** is located in a House Office Building or Senate Office Building;
  - **(2)** was previously used by a Member of Congress who became President; and
  - **(3)** was used as the final individual Member office room of such Member.
- **(b)** *Contents* A plaque installed pursuant to subsection (a) shall—
  - **(1)** commemorate the use of the office by a Member of Congress who became President; and
  - **(2)** provide such information about the use of the office as the Architect of the Capitol considers appropriate.
