# H.R. 4347

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to include veterans in the military adaptive sports program of the Department of Defense.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Warrior Act”.

## § 2. Inclusion of veterans in military adaptive sports program

Section 2564a of title 10, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)(1), by inserting “and veterans” after “members of the armed forces”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (b)(1), by inserting “and veterans” after “members”.
