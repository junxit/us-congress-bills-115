# H.R. 4312

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to ensure the Secretary of Veterans Affairs permits the display of Battlefield Crosses in national cemeteries.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fallen Warrior Battlefield Cross Memorial Act”.

## § 2. Display of Battlefield Crosses in national cemeteries

Section 2403 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection:

> - **(d)**
>   - **(1)** The Secretary may not prohibit the display of a Battlefield Cross in any national cemetery.
>   - **(2)** In this subsection, the term “Battlefield Cross” means a memorial monument in honor of fallen members of the Armed Forces that may include a replica of an inverted rifle, boots, helmets, and identification tag.
