# H.R. 5530

> To amend the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 to repeal desert terminal lakes assistance.

## § 1. Repeal of desert terminal lakes assistance

Section 2507 of the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 (16 U.S.C. 3839bb–6) is repealed.
