# H.R. 348

> To more accurately identify and transfer subsurface gravel sources originally intended to be made available to the Ukpeagvik Inupiat Corporation in exchange for its relinquishment of related property rights.

## § 1. Technical amendment

- **(a)** *Short title* This section may be cited as the “Ukpeagvik Inupiat Corporation Gravel Exchange Amendment Act”.
- **(b)** *Identification and transfer of gravel sources* Section 3 of the Barrow Gas Field Transfer Act of 1984 (Public Law 98–366, 98 Stat. 468) is amended by adding at the end the following: “The foregoing conveyance to UIC shall include all right, title, and interest held by the United States to sand and gravel deposits underlying the surface estate owned by UIC contiguous to the Barrow gas fields, more particularly described as the sand and gravel deposits underlying that UIC surface estate lying within T 21N R16W, sections 7, 17–18, 19–21, and 28–29; T 21N R17W, sections 1–2, and 11–14; T 22N, R18W, sections 4, 9, and 29–32; and T 22N R19W, sections 25 and 36.”.
