# S. 1644

> To clarify the status of the Captain John Smith Chesapeake National Historic Trail as a unit of the National Park System.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Captain John Smith Chesapeake National Historic Trail Parity Act”.

## § 2. Captain John Smith Chesapeake National Historic Trail

Section 5(a)(25)(C) of the National Trails System Act (16 U.S.C. 1244(a)(25)(C)) is amended, in the matter preceding clause (i), by inserting “as a unit of the National Park System” after “the Secretary of the Interior”.
