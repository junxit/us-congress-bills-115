# H.J. RES. 56

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the final rule of the Bureau of Land Management relating to “Onshore Oil and Gas Operations; Federal and Indian Oil and Gas Leases; Site Security”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Bureau of Land Management relating to “Onshore Oil and Gas Operations; Federal and Indian Oil and Gas Leases; Site Security” (81 Fed. Reg. 81356 (November 17, 2016)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
