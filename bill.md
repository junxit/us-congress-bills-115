# H.R. 6414

> To amend title 23, United States Code, to extend the deadline for promulgation of regulations under the tribal transportation self-governance program.

## § 1. Extension of deadline for promulgation of regulations under tribal transportation self-governance program

Section 207(n)(1) of title 23, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (B) by striking “21 months” and inserting “42 months”; and
- **(2)** in subparagraph (C) by striking “30 months” and inserting “48 months”.
