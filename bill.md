# S. 1389

> To allow the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection to provide greater protection to servicemembers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Military Consumer Enforcement Act”.

## § 2. Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection

Section 1002(12) of the Consumer Financial Protection Act of 2010 (12 U.S.C. 5481(12)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (Q), by striking “; and” and inserting a semicolon;
- **(2)** in subparagraph (R), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(S)** sections 101, 106, 107 (except with respect to bailments), 108 (except with respect to insurance), 201 (except with respect to child custody proceedings), 207, 301, 302, 303, 305, and 305A of the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (50 U.S.C. 3911, 3917, 3918, 3919, 3931, 3937, 3951, 3952, 3953, 3955, and 3956).
