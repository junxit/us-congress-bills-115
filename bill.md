# S. 3652

> To support the peaceful resolution of the civil war in Yemen, to address the resulting humanitarian crisis, and to hold the perpetrators responsible for murdering a Saudi dissident.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Saudi Arabia Accountability and Yemen Act of 2018”.
