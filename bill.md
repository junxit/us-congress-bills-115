# H.R. 3475

> To amend title 23, United States Code, to permit certain vehicles to continue to operate on an Interstate System route in the State of Kentucky.

## § 1. Operation of vehicles on certain Kentucky highways

Section 127(l)(2) of title 23, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(C)** The William H. Natcher Parkway (to be designated as a spur of Interstate Route 65) from Interstate Route 65 in Bowling Green, Kentucky, to United States Route 60 in Owensboro, Kentucky.
