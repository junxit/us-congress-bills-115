# H.R. 4801

> To require the Secretary of Transportation to appoint a representative from the Department of Transportation to the board of trustees of the Gateway Program Development Corporation.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Get on Board to Fix the Tunnel Act”.

## § 2. Appointment of representative

Not later than 30 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Transportation shall appoint a representative of the Department of Transportation to the board of trustees of the Gateway Program Development Corporation.
