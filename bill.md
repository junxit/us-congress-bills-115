# S. 1472

> To reauthorize the Tennessee Civil War Heritage Area.

## § 1. Reauthorization of the Tennessee Civil War Heritage Area

Section 208 of division II of the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 (Public Law 104–333; 110 Stat. 4248; 127 Stat. 420; 128 Stat. 314; 129 Stat. 2551) is amended by striking “2017” and inserting “2021”.
