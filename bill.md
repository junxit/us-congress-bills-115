# H.R. 3649

> To amend the Food Security Act of 1985 with respect to annual estimates under the conservation reserve program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fair CRP Payments Act”.

## § 2. Annual estimates

Section 1234(d)(5)(A) of the Food Security Act of 1985 (16 U.S.C. 3834(d)(5)(A)) is amended by striking “every other year” and inserting “every year”.
