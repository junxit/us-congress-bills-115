# H.J. RES. 39

> Disapproving a rule submitted by the Department of Health and Human Services relating to “Compliance with Title X Requirements by Project Recipients in Selecting Subrecipients”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Health and Human Services relating to “Compliance with Title X Requirements by Project Recipients in Selecting Subrecipients” (81 Fed. Reg. 91852), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
