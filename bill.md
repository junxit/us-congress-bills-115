# H.R. 3935

> To provide for an extension of funding for the National Health Service Corps.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Bolstering Organizations and Options to Support Training in Primary Care Act” or the “BOOST Primary Care Act”.

## § 2. Extension for National Health Service Corps

Section 10503(b)(2)(E) of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (42 U.S.C. 254b–2(b)(2)(E)) is amended by striking “2017” and inserting “2019”.
