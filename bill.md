# S. 381

> To repeal the Act entitled “An Act to confer jurisdiction on the State of Iowa over offenses committed by or against Indians on the Sac and Fox Indian Reservation”.

## § 1. Repeal

The Act entitled “An Act to confer jurisdiction on the State of Iowa over offenses committed by or against Indians on the Sac and Fox Indian Reservation”, approved June 30, 1948 (62 Stat. 1161, chapter 759), is repealed.
