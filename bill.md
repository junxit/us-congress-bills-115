# H.R. 5584

> To extend the authority of the Secretary of the Interior to carry out the Equus Beds Division of the Wichita Project.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Wichita Project Equus Beds Division Authorization Extension Act”.

## § 2. Equus beds division

Section 10(h) of Public Law 86–787 (74 Stat. 1026; 120 Stat. 1474) is amended by striking “10 years” and inserting “20 years”.
