# H.J. RES. 143

> Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2019, and for other purposes.

That division C of Public Law 115–245 is amended—

- **(1)** by striking the date specified in section 105(3) and inserting “December 21, 2018”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end (before the short title) the following:
  > ## § 136.
  >
  > Sections 1309(a) and 1319 of the National Flood Insurance Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 4016(a) and 4026) shall be applied by substituting the date specified in section 105(3) of this Act for “December 7, 2018”.
