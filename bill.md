# H.R. 3263

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to extend the Medicare independence at home medical practice demonstration program.

## § 1. Extending the Medicare independence at home demonstration program

Section 1866E of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395cc–5) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (e)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (1), by striking “5-year” and inserting “7-year”; and
  - **(B)** in paragraph (5), by striking “10,000” and inserting “15,000”;
- **(2)** in subsection (g), in the first sentence, by inserting “, including, to the extent practicable, with respect to the use of electronic health information systems, as described in subsection (b)(1)(A)(vi)” after “under the demonstration program”; and
- **(3)** in subsection (i)(1)(A), by striking “will not receive an incentive payment for the second of 2” and inserting “did not achieve savings for the third of 3”.
