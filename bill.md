# S. 1388

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to repeal the requirement for voting assistance officers for members of the Armed Forces.

## § 1. Repeal of requirement for voting assistance officers for members of the Armed Forces

Section 1566 of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking subsection (f).
