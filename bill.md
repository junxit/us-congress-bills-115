# H.R. 3763

> To require a per capita calculation for eligibility of a jurisdiction to participate in the EPSCoR program of the National Science Foundation, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Smarter EPSCoR Act”.

## § 2. Per capita calculation for eligibility of a jurisdiction to participate in EPSCoR

In determining the eligibility of a jurisdiction to participate in the Established Program to Stimulate Competitive Research program described under section 517 of National Science Foundation Authorization Act of 2010 (42 U.S.C. 1862p–9), the Director of the National Science Foundation shall ensure that the proportion of the total amount of National Science Foundation research support attributed to a jurisdiction over a 3-year period is calculated on a per capita basis.
