# H.R. 5667

> To amend the Agricultural Act of 2014 to reauthorize the Acer Access and Development Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Acer Access and Development Program Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Acer Access and Development Program reauthorized

Section 12306(f) of the Agricultural Act of 2014 (7 U.S.C. 1632c(f)) is amended by striking “2014 through 2018” and inserting “2018 through 2023”.
