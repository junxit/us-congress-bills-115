# S. 2608

> To provide that a risk evaluation and mitigation strategy communication plan may include information about Federal and State prescribing requirements for controlled substances.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reducing Opioid Risk Act”.

## § 2. Risk evaluation and mitigation strategy communication plans

Section 505–1(e)(3) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 355–1(e)(3)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (B), by adding a semicolon at the end;
- **(2)** in subparagraph (C), by striking “; or” and inserting a semicolon;
- **(3)** in subparagraph (D), by striking the period and inserting “; or”; and
- **(4)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(E)** disseminating information to health care providers about Federal and State prescribing requirements for controlled substances, including the requirements under section 309(f) of the Controlled Substances Act, as applicable.
