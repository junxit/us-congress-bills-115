# H.R. 1767

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to discontinue certain administrative cost allowances, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Administrative cost allowances

Section 489(a) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1096(a)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking the first sentence; and
- **(2)** by striking “In addition, an” and inserting “An”.
