# H.R. 6625

> To amend the Truth in Lending Act to modify the definition of credit sale.

## § 1. Definition of credit sale

Section 103(h) of the Truth in Lending Act (15 U.S.C. 1602(h)) is amended by inserting “(unless terminable without penalty at any time by the consumer)” after “or lease”.
