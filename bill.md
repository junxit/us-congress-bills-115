# H.R. 4652

> To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to make permanent the Tennessee disproportionate share hospital (DSH) allotment under the Medicaid program.

## § 1. Making permanent Tennessee Medicaid DSH allotment

Section 1923(f)(6)(A)(vi) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396r–4(f)(6)(A)(vi)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the heading, by striking “fiscal years 2015 through 2025” and inserting “fiscal year 2015 and each subsequent fiscal year”; and
- **(2)** by striking “through fiscal year 2025”.
