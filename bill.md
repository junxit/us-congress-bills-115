# H.R. 1514

> To provide reforms through the Organic Act of Guam.

## § 1. Prohibition on salary changes for the Governor, Lieutenant Governor, and Senator of the Guam Legislature

Section 6 of the Organic Act of Guam (48 U.S.C. 1422) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> -  No law varying the compensation for the services of the Governor of Guam, the Lieutenant Governor of Guam, or Senators in the Guam Legislature shall take effect until a General Election of the Guam Legislature shall have intervened.
