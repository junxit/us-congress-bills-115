# H.R. 6848

> To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to ensure that certain Department of Veterans Affairs medical centers have physical locations for the disposal of controlled substances medications.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “VA Safe Opioid Return Act”.

## § 2. Requirement for certain Department of Veterans Affairs medical centers to have physical location for the disposal of controlled substances medications

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall ensure that each covered Department medical center has a physical location where patients may dispose of controlled substances medications.
- **(b)** *Covered Department medical centers* In this section, the term “covered Department medical center” means a medical center with—
  - **(1)** an on-site pharmacy; or
  - **(2)** a law enforcement officer.
