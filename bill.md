# H.R. 3657

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to authorize the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to provide headstones and markers for the graves of spouses and children of veterans who are buried in tribal cemeteries.

## § 1. Eligibility of spouses and children of veterans buried in tribal cemeteries for Department of Veterans Affairs headstones and markers

Section 2306(a)(4) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting “or a tribal organization” after “State”.
