# S. 1411

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to provide for a five-year extension of the payment of the special survivor indemnity allowance under the Survivor Benefit Plan.

## § 1. Five-year extension of payment of special survivor indemnity allowance under Survivor Benefit Plan

Section 1450(m) of title 10, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2)(I), by striking “and 2018” and inserting “through 2023”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (6)—
  - **(A)** by striking “May 31, 2018” and inserting “September 30, 2023”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “June 1, 2018” each place it appears and inserting “October 1, 2023”.
