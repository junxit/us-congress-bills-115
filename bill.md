# H. RES. 404

> Expressing the sense of the House of Representatives regarding the need to create a small donor and public finance system for Congressional elections.

## § 1. Providing for small donor and public financing of elections

It is the sense of the House of Representatives that, in order to eliminate the undue influence of money in politics—

- **(1)** Congress should create a small donor and public finance system for Congressional elections, which will ensure that Congress is responsible to voters instead of well-financed special interests; and
- **(2)** Members of Congress should devote their full time to regular order and governing instead of campaign fundraising.
