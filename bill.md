# H.R. 243

> To amend title 54, United States Code, to prohibit the further extension or establishment of national monuments in the State of Nevada except by express authorization of Congress.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Nevada Land Sovereignty Act”.

## § 2. Limitation on further extension or establishment of national monuments in the State of Nevada

Section 320301(d) of title 54, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in the subsection heading, by inserting “or Nevada” after “Wyoming”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “or Nevada” after “Wyoming”.
