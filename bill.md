# H.R. 5675

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to require prescription drug plan sponsors under the Medicare program to establish drug management programs for at-risk beneficiaries.

## § 1. Requiring prescription drug plan sponsors under Medicare to establish drug management programs for at-risk beneficiaries

Section 1860D–4(c) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395w–104(c)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1), by inserting after subparagraph (E) the following new subparagraph:
  > - **(F)** With respect to plan years beginning on or after January 1, 2021, a drug management program for at-risk beneficiaries described in paragraph (5).

- **(2)** in paragraph (5)(A), by inserting “(and for plan years beginning on or after January 1, 2021, a PDP sponsor shall)” after “A PDP sponsor may”.
