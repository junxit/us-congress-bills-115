# H. CON. RES. 148

> Directing the Secretary of the Senate to make a correction in the enrollment of the bill S. 3628.

That in the enrollment of the bill S. 3628, the Secretary of the Senate shall make the following correction: Strike “November 30, 2018” each place such term appears and insert “December 7, 2018”.
