# S. 1223

> To repeal the Klamath Tribe Judgment Fund Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Klamath Tribe Judgment Fund Repeal Act ”.

## § 2. Repeal

Public Law 89–224 (commonly know as the “Klamath Tribe Judgment Fund Act”) (79 Stat. 897) is repealed.
