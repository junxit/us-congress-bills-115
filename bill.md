# S. 535

> To amend the Water Infrastructure Improvements for the Nation Act to make applicable to the State of South Dakota a provision relating to certain Bureau of Reclamation permit fees.

## § 1. Bureau of Reclamation Dakotas Area Office permit fees for cabins and trailers

Section 3601 of the Water Infrastructure Improvements for the Nation Act (Public Law 114–322) is amended by inserting “or the State of South Dakota” after “North Dakota”.
