# S. 1144

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to encourage business creation by allowing faster recovery of start-up and organizational expenses, to simplify accounting methods for small businesses, to expand expensing and provide accelerated cost recovery to encourage investment in new plants and equipment, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; amendment of 1986 Code; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Investment in New Ventures and Economic Success Today Act of 2017” or the “INVEST Act of 2017”.
- **(b)** *Amendment of 1986 Code* Except as otherwise expressly provided, whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
