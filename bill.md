# S. 2441

> To amend the Steel Industry American Heritage Area Act of 1996 to repeal the funding limitation.

## § 1. Rivers of Steel National Heritage Area

Section 409(a) of the Steel Industry American Heritage Area Act of 1996 (54 U.S.C. 320101 note; 110 Stat. 4256; 113 Stat. 1501A–158; 122 Stat. 824; 129 Stat. 2551) is amended by striking the second sentence.
